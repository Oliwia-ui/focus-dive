# Product Vision

## Promise

Focus Dive helps people begin and finish focused work by turning elapsed time into a calm visual ascent. The timer should feel like a quiet instrument, not another productivity dashboard demanding attention.

## Audience

- Knowledge workers who use time-boxed focus sessions
- People who benefit from visible progress without aggressive gamification
- macOS users who want a focused, native, private tool

## Product principles

1. **Calm over urgency** — motion, copy, and notifications support concentration.
2. **Progress is spatial** — time becomes depth, ascent, light, and surfacing.
3. **One mission at a time** — the current task remains the primary context.
4. **Reward consistency lightly** — streaks and discoveries acknowledge work without becoming the work.
5. **Local by default** — core functionality requires no account or network service.
6. **Native and accessible** — macOS conventions, keyboard control, clear labels, and Reduce Motion are baseline behavior.

## MVP outcome

A user can name a mission, complete configurable focus and break sessions, see immediate progress, review completed dives, and return later with settings and history intact.

## Non-goals for the MVP

- Cloud sync, accounts, collaboration, or social features
- Task-manager or calendar integration
- Ambient audio and completion sounds
- Advanced reporting or data export
- Store distribution automation

## Success signals

- A first session can start without setup.
- Timer state remains predictable across start, pause, reset, stop, skip, and completion.
- The interface remains legible at its minimum window size.
- The app can be used entirely from the keyboard for primary actions.
- Saved settings and completed dives survive relaunch.
